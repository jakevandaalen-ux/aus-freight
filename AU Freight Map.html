Australia Post zones come from Australia Post's MyPost Business postcode list: Metro = Capital City (the eight capitals), Outer metro = Major Areas (e.g. Gold Coast, Newcastle, Geelong, Launceston), Regional = Rural (each state's "Outer" postcodes), plus Remote Islands & Locations. Australia Post notes that TAS Other postcodes don't get Zone 1 pricing. Postcode locations and remoteness (ABS Remoteness Areas 2021) come from the Australian Postcodes dataset; each dot sits at the average position of the postcode's suburbs, and PO-box-only postcodes are left out. State outlines are simplified from Natural Earth via world-geojson. The ACT sits inside the NSW outline. Warehouses are placed at their suburb's centre, and "postcodes" beside each one counts the postcodes it is closest to in a straight line (road distance will differ).
